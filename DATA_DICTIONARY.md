# Data dictionary and workbook guide

## `README`
Overview of the dataset, analytical scope, interpretation, evidence-linkage approach, and workbook structure.

## `Country_Virus_Data`
Primary analytical table containing 780 records (195 sovereign states × four viruses). It includes transmission category, vector status, vaccine-access category, disaggregated vaccine-access variables, evidence/source identifiers, confidence classification, linkage status, and explanatory notes.

## `Vector_Data`
Jurisdiction-level vector classifications for Aedes and Culex vector groups, including source identifiers and interpretation notes.

## `Summary`
Descriptive counts and percentages by virus and transmission category. Jurisdictions are weighted equally.

## `Definitions`
Operational definitions for transmission, vaccine access, coverage, weighting, vector status, and source linkage.

## `Analytical_Decisions`
Prespecified analytical decisions and rules used to harmonise classifications.

## `Evidence_Assessment`
Operational criteria for evidence confidence and source-linkage status.

## `Source_Inventory`
Source identifiers, source titles, source types, scope, URLs, and access dates supporting the dataset.

## `Original_195`
Preserved analytical input table for the 195-sovereign-state scope.

## `Original_249`
Preserved broader country/territory table for sensitivity analyses.

## Important interpretation notes

1. Regulatory approval, market availability, programme implementation, and vaccination coverage are distinct concepts.
2. Country-level proportions do not represent population-weighted vaccine access or individual protection.
3. Vector presence indicates ecological compatibility and does not establish virus circulation.
4. Imported cases do not demonstrate local transmission.
5. Source identifiers should be interpreted using the accompanying `Source_Inventory`.
6. The assessment cutoff is 6 May 2026.
